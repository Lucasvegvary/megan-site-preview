About Megan — Intuitive Reader, Energy Healer & Spiritual Guide

There are readers who turn the unseen into a spectacle, and skeptics who wave it away entirely. Megan is the rare middle: she'll take you all the way out, then bring you all the way back down with one clear thing to hold. Feet on the earth. Eyes on the stars.

Her work pairs honest messages from Spirit with practical, grounded support — so you don't just feel held, you feel moved forward, with something real to carry into Monday morning. No fear. No theatrics. No fishing.

Who is Megan? Megan is a grounded intuitive reader, energy healer and spiritual guide. She connects with Spirit, your angels and your guides to bring through clear, honest guidance — and helps you release what no longer serves you through cord-cutting and energy healing. She holds the rare middle ground between sensational psychics and dismissive skeptics: cosmic reach, grounded delivery, never fear-based, always something practical to carry home. Sessions are offered online worldwide and in person at her next SoulSearch Enlightenment Expo.

Her Story

A guide who keeps both feet on the ground

Megan knew she was different as a child. Her first real awakening came in high school, through premonition dreams — glimpses that arrived before she had any framework to make sense of them.

The turning point came during one of the hardest chapters of her life. After becoming homeless with her young son, she went looking for a different way to heal — and found the Reno Psychic Institute. There she stopped fighting what she was, learned to work with it, and uncovered gifts she now considers hers to share. She has trained and studied there for about five years.

She chose the grounded path on purpose. Having lived so much of her life in fight-or-flight, she knows from the inside that grounding isn't a nicety — it's how the body feels safe enough to be present and make clear, honest decisions. So she takes the unseen completely seriously, and keeps both feet firmly on the earth.

Her view of this work is refreshingly simple: it doesn't put anyone in a box. "It's like water — it flows, and anyone can go with it, as long as they have faith and belief in themselves." What she loves most is watching people grow into the best versions of themselves.

“Guided by Spirit. Rooted in Love. Here to help you remember who you are — and where you're going.”

An Honest Promise

How Megan works — and what she'll never do

What she will do

  • Meet you with warmth, zero judgement, and complete privacy
  • Tell you the truth, kindly — not what you want to hear

  • Hand your power back to you — take what resonates, leave the rest

What she'll never do

  • Never tell you you're cursed, or sell you a "cleansing" to fix it

  • Never pressure you to book, believe, or come back

  • Never make you feel silly for being skeptical
