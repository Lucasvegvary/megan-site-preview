Cord-Cutting

What Is a Cord-Cutting Session? A Gentle Guide to Releasing Energetic Cords

By Megan · Intuitive, Healer & Guide

A cord-cutting session is a gentle, intentional energy-healing practice for releasing the draining energetic ties — "cords" — that keep you tethered to a person, relationship, pattern, or past version of yourself. It clears what no longer serves you while leaving your love, your good connections, and your free will fully intact, so you feel lighter, clearer, and more yourself. It can be done online from anywhere or in person.

If you've ever felt drained after spending time with a certain person, or noticed you simply can't stop thinking about a relationship that's already over, you may be carrying an energetic cord. A cord-cutting session is a calm, grounded way to release those ties — no fear, no theatrics — so you can set down what you've been quietly carrying and walk away feeling lighter, clearer, and more like yourself again.

What are energetic cords?

Energetic cords are the invisible ties of energy we form with the people, places, patterns and past versions of ourselves that we're connected to. Some cords are nourishing — the warm, two-way bond between you and someone you love. Others quietly drain you, keeping you tethered to something that has run its course.

We form cords naturally through closeness, intensity and time. That's not a problem in itself. The challenge comes when a cord keeps pulling on your energy long after the connection stopped serving your highest good — like a tab left open in the background, quietly using up your power.

Signs you may be ready for a cord-cutting

There's no "should" here — only what feels true for you. You stay in the lead: take what resonates and leave the rest. That said, many people notice one or more of these signs before they reach out:

  • You feel drained or heavy after being around a particular person
  • You can't stop replaying a relationship, conversation or chapter that's over
  • You keep repeating the same pattern and can't quite step out of it
  • A relationship ended, but it doesn't feel emotionally or energetically complete
  • You feel stuck, foggy, or like part of your energy is "somewhere else"
  • You're ready for a fresh start and want to clear the way for it

What actually happens in a session

A cord-cutting session is calm, gentle and always in your control. While every session is shaped around you, the shape often looks like this:

We identify what's ready to release

Together we get grounded and gently name the cords and attachments that are weighing on you.

The cords are lovingly released

With intention and care, the draining ties are cleared — never your love, and never your free will.

You're filled back in with your own light

We close by restoring and grounding your energy, so you leave feeling whole, spacious and clear.

How you might feel afterward

Most people describe feeling noticeably lighter, calmer and more themselves. Sometimes there's a wave of relief; sometimes a tender, settling feeling as things integrate over the next day or two. To support the shift, it helps to drink water, rest, move gently, and give yourself a little quiet space to journal or simply breathe. Cord-cutting often pairs naturally with broader energy healing, which clears and rebalances your whole field rather than one specific tie.

A few honest reassurances

It won't force anyone out of your life. Cord-cutting releases the draining energetic tie — not your love, and not your choice about whether to keep someone close. It won't sever your good connections. Only what no longer serves you is released; the nourishing bonds remain. And it works beautifully online, because energy isn't limited by distance.

Cord-cutting is often one of the first releases people reach for when something larger is shifting in them. If you've been feeling a deeper change stirring, you may also recognize yourself in the signs of a spiritual awakening.
