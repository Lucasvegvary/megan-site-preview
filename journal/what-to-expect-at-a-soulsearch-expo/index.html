What to Expect at a SoulSearch Enlightenment Expo (and How to Find Megan)

By Megan · Intuitive, Healer & Guide

A SoulSearch Enlightenment Expo is a welcoming weekend gathering — part psychic fair, part healing fair, part holistic marketplace — where you can sit with intuitive readers and energy healers, explore wellbeing vendors, and attend talks. You buy admission, then choose who you'd like to spend time with. It's open to the curious and the seasoned alike, and you stay in the lead the entire time.

If you've ever wondered what actually happens at a psychic fair or healing fair, here's an honest, grounded look — no mystery, no pressure. Whether it's your first expo or your fifteenth, you can walk in knowing what to expect, how to choose well, and how to find me there.

What a SoulSearch Enlightenment Expo actually is

At its heart, a SoulSearch Enlightenment Expo is a gathering of people who take inner growth seriously. Under one roof you'll find intuitive readers offering short sessions, hands-on energy healers, and vendors sharing crystals, books, art, oils, jewelry and other tools for the wellbeing-minded. Many expos also host talks and demonstrations throughout the day, so you can simply sit, listen and absorb between sessions.

You don't need to "believe" a certain way to belong there. The room is full of thoughtful adults at a crossroads or an awakening — people who want depth without drama. That's exactly who I tend to meet at these events, and it's a good crowd to be among.

What to expect when you walk in

You'll typically pay an admission at the door, receive a map or program, and then the day is yours. Readings and healing sessions are usually first-come, first-served at each practitioner's table, paid for separately and directly. Sessions are often short — long enough for a clear, focused takeaway, not an all-afternoon commitment. Here's the honest rhythm of a good expo:

  • You browse freely and never have to commit before you're ready
  • You choose who to sit with — no one is assigned to you
  • You bring your own questions, and you decide how much to share
  • You take what resonates and leave the rest — always
  • You leave with something usable, not just something dazzling

A grounded practitioner will never trade in fear, never pressure you toward an expensive "fix," and never make your peace conditional on coming back. If anything ever feels theatrical or fear-based, you're free to thank them and move on. Trusting that instinct is part of the work.

How to choose a reader or healer (a few honest tips)

With so many tables, choosing can feel like the hardest part. It doesn't have to be. A few grounded pointers:

Notice how you feel near their table

Before a word is spoken, your body has an opinion. If you feel calmer and more curious, that's a good sign. If you feel rushed or uneasy, keep walking.

Come with a real question

You don't need a perfect one. "I feel stuck and I'm not sure why" is plenty. A clear intention gives the session somewhere meaningful to go.

Listen for the takeaway

The best sessions leave you with something you can actually use — a next step, a fresh perspective, a knot loosened — not just a list of predictions.

First-timer tips for a smooth, grounded day

A little preparation makes the whole experience more nourishing. None of this is required — it simply helps:

  • Arrive earlier rather than later; popular readers fill up as the day goes on
  • Bring cash as well as cards — some tables prefer cash for quick sessions
  • Wear comfortable shoes and pace yourself; an expo is a lot of energy in one room
  • Bring water and take grounding breaks between sessions
  • Jot down what lands while it's fresh, so you can sit with it later
  • Keep an open, easy mind — you're allowed to be curious without committing

If you're new to readings altogether, you might also like my overview of what an intuitive reading is and how it works before you arrive. It'll help you walk up to any table — mine or anyone else's — with a clearer sense of what you're looking for.
