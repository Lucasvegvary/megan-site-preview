What is a cord-cutting session? The Weight You Carry

What are energetic cords?

Energetic cords are the invisible ties we form to the people, places, patterns and past versions of ourselves we've been deeply connected to. Some of these cords are nourishing — they carry love, support and warmth back and forth, and they help us feel held. Those are beautiful, and they get to stay.

Others quietly drain us. Sometimes it's a cord you've carried a long time that simply no longer serves you — an old relationship, a chapter that ended but never felt complete, a pattern you keep repeating. Sometimes it's a fresh one, formed in something recent, keeping you stuck in pain you can't quite move through. Either way, it can leave a cord pulling at your energy long after the moment has passed, like a tab left open in the background using up your power. Cord-cutting is the gentle work of releasing those draining ties, so your energy returns fully to you and you stop carrying weight that was never yours to keep. There's nothing to fear here, and you never lose the love — only the heaviness.

Integration

After your session

Most people leave a cord-cutting session feeling noticeably lighter and more spacious, as though something heavy has finally been set down. Sometimes you may also feel a little tender as things settle — that's normal, and it's part of the release moving through. Be gentle with yourself in the day or two that follow, and let your energy reorganize at its own pace.

If you'd like to keep the spaciousness open and supported, gentle energy healing after your session is a beautiful way to help everything settle and rebalance.

A few simple things help the shift land:

  • Drink plenty of water — release moves through the body, too.
  • Give yourself permission to rest and slow down.
  • Journal anything that rises — feelings, memories, clarity, relief.
  • Notice the new spaciousness, and let yourself fill it on your own terms.
  • Be tender with yourself if old feelings stir as everything settles.
