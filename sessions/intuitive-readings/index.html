What is an intuitive reading? An intuitive reading is a calm conversation in which an intuitive senses the energy of your situation and brings through clear, usable guidance — drawing on her spirit guides, her collective team and the angels. Rather than predicting fixed fate, it helps you see a question or crossroads honestly — never fear-based — so you can choose your next step from a grounded, reconnected place.

The Question Everyone Asks

Intuitive reading vs psychic reading

If you've ever felt caught between sensational psychics and dismissive skeptics, this is the middle you've been looking for: cosmic reach, grounded delivery. Megan offers both, in the same grounded voice.

The two overlap, and people use the words interchangeably — but the difference is usually in delivery. A psychic reading often centers on predicting fixed events: this will happen, that person is your soulmate, here's your fate. A grounded reading is less about prophecy and more about perception — sensing the energy of your situation and reflecting it back so you can actually do something with it.

That difference matters. Nothing is handed to you as locked-in destiny, and nothing is designed to frighten you or keep you coming back. You stay in the lead the entire time: you take what resonates and leave the rest, and you always walk away with a clear, usable takeaway rather than a head full of fear.

Mediumship

When a loved one chooses to come in

If a departed loved one chooses to come in during your reading, Megan can offer mediumship — tender, unhurried and held with care.

When they come, Megan can see them, feel their love for you, and pass that energy on so you can feel it too — along with any guidance or answers they bring through. It's one of the most tender parts of this work.

She can't summon anyone on demand. It only happens if they choose to come. But when they do, it tends to land as exactly what your heart needed — a reminder that the love doesn't end, and that it's never really goodbye.
