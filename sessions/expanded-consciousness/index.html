What is an expanded consciousness session? An expanded consciousness session is a calm, guided look at the bigger picture of your life — higher perspective, universal wisdom, and multidimensional or galactic themes — explored with no fear and no theatrics. Megan tunes in, shares honestly, and always leaves you with a usable takeaway for your real life.

Feet On The Earth. Eyes On The Stars.

A grounded approach to the bigger questions

Some questions are simply bigger than the everyday — about your soul's path, your sense of meaning, and whether there is more to your story than this one life. There is a sensational, fear-flavored version of this conversation out there, and there is a dismissive, eye-rolling one. This is neither. It is the trusted middle: cosmic reach with grounded delivery, where the bigger picture is met calmly and honestly.

Even when we touch expansive, "cosmic" themes, the work stays non-dogmatic and rooted. This is not about prediction, hype or theatrics — it is about higher perspective, clarity and integration. Whatever comes through, we bring it gently back to your real life, so you leave with something thoughtful and genuinely useful, not just something to wonder about.

Starseed Reading

"Am I a starseed?" — a calm place to ask

If you've always felt a little different — like part of you remembers somewhere else — you may have quietly wondered am I a starseed. A starseed reading doesn't rush to hand you a label or a dramatic story. It's a grounded exploration of whether your soul feels connected to a galactic or multidimensional origin, and what that might gently mean for how you live now.

You stay in the lead the whole way through. Take what resonates, leave the rest — there is never pressure to believe anything. If you'd like to reflect before we talk, you can start with the journal piece Am I a Starseed? A Grounded Look.
