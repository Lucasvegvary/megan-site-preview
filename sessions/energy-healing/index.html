Integration

After your session

Most people leave an energy healing session feeling noticeably lighter and more spacious, as though something heavy has finally been set down. Sometimes you may also feel a little tender or quiet as things settle — that's normal, and it's part of the rebalancing moving through. Be gentle with yourself in the day or two that follow.

A few simple things help the shift land:

  • Drink plenty of water — energy moves through the body, too.
  • Give yourself permission to rest and slow down.
  • Journal anything that rises — feelings, clarity, relief.
  • Notice the new lightness, and let yourself fill it on your own terms.
  • Keep working with your doctor or therapist for any health concerns.
